About Balaji High School

Balaji High School was established in 2007 and is situated in the rural heart of Santhamaguluru Block, Prakasam District, Andhra Pradesh.

The institution provides secondary education from Grade VI through Grade X in a supportive co-educational learning environment. Telugu is the medium of instruction, ensuring strong regional language foundations paired with English and modern Sciences.

Our vision is to nurture responsible, confident, and capable young individuals who can contribute positively to society, agriculture, technology, and public leadership.
